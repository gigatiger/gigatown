So You Think You Could Be Trans?
Basics
    How do I know if I'm transgender?
    • Personally, I've known I was a man my whole life. A lot of trans people experience 'gender dysphoria', or a feeling ofdisconnect between the gender one was assigned at birth and one's true gender identity. No one can tell if you're truly trans expect for you, but we do live in a world where there's a lot of pressure not to transition. Changing your name and pronouns is framed as an added expectation for others, a burden you're asking of them. (US based perspective) Informed consent can make hormone replacement therapy easier to access, but often times trans healthcare requires the validation of doctors and psyuchiatrists. I'm not arguing the benefits of medical intervention, but explaining why I'd be careful relying on external validation. We live in a cisnormative world where social and medical transition require effot and vulnerabiliy. For a trans person to survive and thrive, you have to be able to look in the mirror and reckon with what you see, regardless of how strangers will perceive you. I've spent month doubting my own transness. But I've lived for 5 years as a man, and I feel more alive than before. Truth is, I still had 'doubts' in the hours leading up to my top surgery. Here I sit two years later in awe at my own results. I can't give you unbiased advice. I've experienced the euphoria of belonging. I'd tell you to dive in head first. This is one of this things that requires some level of self-guided introspection.

On Transmedicalism
    Do you need dysphoria to be trans?
    • As a trans person 5 years on t, post top surgey, with severe dysphoria...I truly do not think it matters. Transmedicalism involves a fixation on non dysphoric trans people that is not mirrored by real life obstacles that trans people face. If I am pro trans, I will not push the idea that being trans requires gender dysphoria, a mental illness. No one gets a say in the gender identity assigned to them at birth, everyone should have the autonomy to decide whether or not they want to maintain that identity.
